Loot-The-Loop

What is Loot the Loop?

Loot the Loop is a solo route-planning puzzle. You move through a circular temple deck, reveal information gradually, and try to collect all four jewels before escaping.

The deck's top card is your current room. Movement always wraps around the loop, so each action changes what you can safely reach next.

How to play

  • Start with Look Around to flip the top two rooms. This is the only action that reveals new hidden information.
  • Use Explore X where X comes from visible number rooms in the top two cards. Movement counts from the top card.
  • Use Mark Path to save the current top number room (up to 3), then Return a saved path to the top when you need to adjust timing.
  • Use the Rooms shown slider above the board to limit how many cards are rendered, which makes planning easier on smaller screens.
  • Collect 💎 jewels and other face-up loot by landing on them. Avoid 💀 traps. Reach the exit after collecting all four jewels to win.
  • The exit appears as a stone (🪨) until all four jewels are collected. Once you have all jewels, it changes to stairs (🪜) and you can escape by landing on it.
  • Undo is available for actions after the latest reveal, and is reset when you use Look Around.
