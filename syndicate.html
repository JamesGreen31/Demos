Syndicate Demo

Description

Syndicate is a rank-climbing card challenge about infiltrating a five-tier criminal hierarchy. Promote one member at a time from Rank 5 toward Rank 1, then refill the vacated spot with a candidate whose value matches the remaining pair by sum-or-product last-digit rules. If a promotion creates a fourth member in the destination rank, sacrifice one immediately. Win by promoting out of Rank 1, and keep your sacrifice count as low as possible.

How to Play

  • Select a rank that currently has exactly 3 members, then choose one member to promote.
  • Promotions move up one step (R5→R4→R3→R2→R1), and promoting from Rank 1 wins instantly.
  • For ranks 5-2, choose whether each promotion joins the next rank's members or candidates.
  • Use New Game to reshuffle when you want to restart from a fresh deal.
Advanced rules (replacement + overflow)
  • After promoting from a rank, refill that same rank from its candidate column using the two remaining members' values.
  • A candidate is legal only when its value matches (a+b)%10 or (a*b)%10.
  • If the promoted-into rank reaches 4 members, it overflows. You must immediately sacrifice one card there.
  • You lose if no legal promotion remains.
