Shikaku

About the game

Shikaku (also called Rectangles or Divide by Squares) is a Japanese logic puzzle where you partition a grid into rectangles. Each given number tells you the exact area of the rectangle that contains that clue.

The puzzle was popularized by puzzle magazines and books in Japan, and later spread worldwide through newspaper puzzle sections and online logic puzzle collections.

How to play

  • Select a clue by clicking its numbered cell.
  • Pick a start corner (blue), then pick an end corner (gold) to form a rectangle.
  • The selected clue may be anywhere inside the rectangle (not only on a corner).
  • Rectangles must match clue area, contain exactly one clue, and not overlap.
  • If you click a completed clue, its rectangle is removed so you can redraw it.
  • Use Solve if you want the board completed automatically, or Clear Board to retry.
