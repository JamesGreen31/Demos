Shikaku Game (DP Solver)

What is dynamic programming doing?

You play by placing rectangles so each clue cell belongs to one rectangle with matching area. The Solve button runs a memoized state-compression DP over covered cells + used clues.

This keeps the demo game-like while still exposing DP ideas through candidate counts and visited states.

How to play

  • Select a clue by clicking its numbered cell.
  • Click any other cell to form a rectangle between the clue and that target.
  • Rectangles must match clue area, contain exactly one clue, and not overlap.
  • Use Solve for the DP answer, or Clear Board to retry.
