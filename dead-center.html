Dead Center

What is Dead Center?

Dead Center is a solo card-defense puzzle where your 3×3 cabin is surrounded by 12 zombies. Each turn you reveal threats, play a drawn card on a valid cabin pile, then try to kill an adjacent zombie using support cards on the same line.

This demo follows the Isaludo rules and uses the Loot-The-Loop visual style so it reads as a consistent demo package.

How to play

  • Placement: equal value, same-color onto higher value, or opposite-color onto lower value.
  • Aces are 1. Jokers are wild for placement and can be played on any top card.
  • Attack strength is the sum of the two cards behind your play on that same zombie line; it must be 10+.
  • Jack: no suit restriction. Queen: both support cards match zombie color. King: both match suit.
  • Joker support cards count as all suits/colors, but add 0 value.

Rank examples on that support link

  • Jack: sum must be 10+; no color/suit restriction.
  • Queen: sum must be 10+ and both support cards match zombie color (joker counts as matching color).
  • King: sum must be 10+ and both support cards match zombie suit (joker counts as matching suit).
  • Joker support: counts as matching color/suit, but contributes 0 toward the sum.
