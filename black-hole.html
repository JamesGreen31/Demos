Black Hole

Description

Black Hole is a positional-number strategy game played on an upside-down triangular pyramid of circles. Players take turns claiming exactly one open circle and place their own rising number sequence (1, 2, 3, and so on for that player). Once a circle is confirmed, it is permanently locked. When one circle remains, it becomes the black hole. Each player totals only the numbers in circles directly connected to that black hole, and the lowest total wins.

How to Play

  • Choose 2 players (height 6) or 3 players (height 7), then start.
  • Enable AI to play Red vs Blue vs Gold (AI), with Gold always moving last.
  • On each turn, pick one open circle and confirm the move.
  • Each player places increasing numbers: 1, 2, 3, and so on for their own turns.
  • Placed circles lock immediately. No undo moves are allowed.
  • When one circle remains, it becomes the black hole.
  • Add the numbers in circles touching the black hole. Lowest total wins.

Configuration

Choose your player count and start the game.
