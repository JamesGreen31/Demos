How to Play

  • Click line segments to draw your loop.
  • Each clue says exactly how many sides of that cell are in the loop.
  • Loops cannot branch or end; every used dot should have degree 2.

Controls

Lower density gives fewer clues and a harder puzzle. Puzzle numbers are still generator-driven.
