Skyway

Market drafting + grid stacking prototype with blueprint routing.

What is Skyway?

Skyway is a solo sequencing prototype where you draft a visible market stack and place its top blueprint card into a 3×3 build grid. Each placement grows suit-specific runs while preserving strict ascending order.

Your goal is to assemble clean per-suit routes from value 1 upward. The endgame panel tracks each suit's current streak and marks PASS once a suit reaches five connected values.

How to play

  • Select one of the three market blueprint stacks. The selected stack is highlighted.
  • The top card of that stack is your active card for this turn.
  • Drop the active card into any glowing valid cell in the 3×3 play area.
  • A cell is valid when it is empty, or when its top card has the same suit and exactly one lower value.
  • Use Choose stack to cycle through non-empty stacks and Place in cell to auto-place in the first valid cell.
  • Use Reset market to reshuffle market/deck/discard into fresh blueprint stacks.
  • Undo reverts the latest action snapshot, and New game starts from a brand-new shuffled setup.
